The Problem

Students often go to the library to borrow a book and find out it is already taken. They also forget the return date and pay late fees, and they have to stand in a long line at the desk just to ask if a book is available.

Example: a student needs a database textbook for the midterm, walks to the library, and finds that all copies are borrowed.

Benefits of BookNest

  • Check if a book is available before going to the library.
  • Reserve a book online and pick it up later.
  • See the return date of every borrowed book.
  • Save time and avoid late fees.

How It Works

  1. Sign up with your student ID.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Search for the book you need.
  4. Reserve it and collect it from the library desk.

Our three main services

1. Search books
2. Reserve online
3. Track return dates
