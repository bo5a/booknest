About BookNest

Our idea | Our goals | FAQ | Our team

Our Idea

BookNest is a simple website for the university library. It lets students see which books are available, reserve a book online, and remember when they need to return it. We chose this idea because we faced the same problem ourselves before exams.

Our Goals

  • Make borrowing books faster and easier.
  • Reduce crowding at the library desk.
  • Help students avoid late fees.
